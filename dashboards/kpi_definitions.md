# KPI Definitions

## Total Bookings

Total number of reservation records.

Formula:
COUNT(*)

---

## Cancellation Rate

Cancelled bookings divided by total bookings.

Formula:
(Cancelled Bookings / Total Bookings) × 100

---

## Average ADR

Average Daily Rate across bookings.

Formula:
Average(adr)

---

## Total Room Nights

Sum of total_nights.

Formula:
SUM(total_nights)

---

## Average Lead Time

Average number of days between booking date and arrival date.

Formula:
Average(lead_time)

---

## Segment Share

Percentage contribution of each customer or market segment to total bookings.

Formula:
(Segment Bookings / Total Bookings) × 100
